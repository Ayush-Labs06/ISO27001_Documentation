**ISO 27001:2022 — Clause 5.2 | Version 1.0**
**Owner:** CEO / Top Management

**Review Date:** Annual

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes [Organization Name]'s commitment to information security and provides the framework within which the Information Security Management System (ISMS) operates. It applies to all information assets, personnel, systems, and processes within the ISMS scope.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All employees (full-time, part-time, and temporary)
- All contractors and third parties with access to [Organization Name]'s information assets
- All systems, cloud infrastructure, applications, and data within the ISMS scope

---

## 3. Information Security Commitment

[Organization Name] is committed to:

1. **Protecting information assets** — We protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all information entrusted to us by customers, partners, employees, and the organization itself.

2. **Legal and regulatory compliance** — We comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and contractual obligations relating to information security, including GDPR and any sector-specific requirements.

3. **Risk-based decision making** — Information security decisions are based on formal risk assessment. We implement controls proportionate to the risk.

4. **Continual improvement** — We continuously improve our ISMS based on performance measurement, audit findings, incident learnings, and changing threat landscape.

5. **Security as everyone's responsibility** — Every person with access to [Organization Name]'s systems and information has a responsibility to protect it. Security is not just an IT function.

## 6. Policy Principles

### 6.1 Confidentiality
Information is accessed only by those with a legitimate business need. All information assets are classified and handled according to the Data Classification Policy.

### 6.2 Integrity
Information is accurate, complete, and protected from unauthorized modification. Changes to critical systems and data are controlled and auditable.

### 6.3 Availability
Information and systems are available when needed. Business continuity and disaster recovery plans protect against disruption.

### 6.4 Legal Compliance
[Organization Name] complies with all applicable information security legislation, regulations, and contractual obligations. The legal and regulatory register is maintained by [Legal Counsel / ISMS Owner].

### 6.5 Risk Management
Risks to information assets are formally assessed and treated. The Risk Register and Risk Treatment Plan define how risks are managed. Risk acceptance decisions require approval from the ISMS Owner and, for critical risks, the CEO.

### 6.6 Incident Management
All suspected or confirmed information security incidents must be reported immediately using the process defined in the Incident Response Policy. No incident should go unreported due to fear of blame.

### 6.7 Access Control
Access to information and systems is granted on a least-privilege basis, authorized by appropriate management, and reviewed quarterly. Access is revoked within 24 hours of employment termination.

### 6.8 Third Parties
Suppliers and third parties with access to [Organization Name]'s information are subject to security assessment and contractual security obligations per the Supplier Security Policy.

## 8. Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy or any supporting ISMS policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. Suspected criminal activity will be referred to appropriate authorities.

---

## 9. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually, or following:
- A significant security incident
- Major organizational or technological change
- Changes in legal or regulatory requirements
- Management review outputs recommending revision
