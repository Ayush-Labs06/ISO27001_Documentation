## How to Use This Tracker

- Add a row per employee/contractor in Section 1.
- Mark each training module with ✓ (complete), ✗ (incomplete/overdue), or N/A (not applicable to role).
- Date format: YYYY-MM-DD.
- Export from LMS monthly; update this record quarterly at minimum.
- Retain completed records for 3 years minimum.
