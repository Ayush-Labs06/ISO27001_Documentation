# Management Review Procedure
**ISO 27001:2022 — Clause 9.3 | Version 1.0**
**Owner:** ISMS Owner
**Approved by:** [CEO] | [Date]

---

## 1. Purpose

Top management must review the ISMS at planned intervals to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness. This procedure defines how [Organization Name] conducts management reviews in conformance with ISO 27001:2022 Clause 9.3.

---

## 2. Who Must Attend

Clause 9.3 requires **top management** participation — not just the ISMS Owner. "Top management" means the CEO (and/or executive board). The ISMS Owner facilitates; the CEO must be present and engaged.

**Required attendees:**
- CEO (top management — mandatory)
- ISMS Owner (facilitator)
- CTO / Technical Lead

**Invited (relevant to specific agenda items):**
- HR Lead (for workforce/training items)
- DevOps Lead (for infrastructure/technical items)
- Legal/DPO (if GDPR or legal compliance items)

---

## 3. Frequency

| Minimum | Recommended |
|---------|-------------|
| Annual (before certification/surveillance audit) | Semi-annual for first 2 years; annual thereafter |

**Trigger for unscheduled review:**
- Major security incident (P1/P2)
- Significant organizational change (merger, pivot, major headcount change)
- Significant change to threat landscape
- Adverse certification audit finding

---

## 4. Mandatory Inputs (Clause 9.3.2)

The following inputs must be considered at every management review. All must be prepared by the ISMS Owner before the meeting:

| Input | ISO 9.3.2 Ref | Prepared By | Source Document |
|-------|--------------|------------|-----------------|
| Status of actions from previous review | 9.3.2(a) | ISMS Owner | Previous review minutes — action tracker |
| Changes in external/internal issues relevant to ISMS | 9.3.2(b) | ISMS Owner | Context analysis update |
| Changes in needs and expectations of interested parties | 9.3.2(b) | ISMS Owner | Stakeholder register update |
| Feedback on IS performance — nonconformities and corrective actions | 9.3.2(c) | ISMS Owner | Corrective action tracker |
| Monitoring and measurement results (KPIs) | 9.3.2(c) | ISMS Owner | KPI dashboard |
| Audit results (internal audit findings) | 9.3.2(c) | Internal Auditor | Internal audit report |
| Fulfilment of IS objectives | 9.3.2(c) | ISMS Owner | IS objectives tracking |
| Results of risk assessment and status of risk treatment | 9.3.2(d) | ISMS Owner | Risk register; risk treatment plan |
| Opportunities for continual improvement | 9.3.2(e) | ISMS Owner | Improvement backlog |

---

## 5. Required Outputs (Clause 9.3.3)

The management review must produce documented outputs (meeting minutes) that include:

- [ ] Decisions and actions related to continual improvement opportunities
- [ ] Any need for changes to the ISMS (policies, procedures, controls, scope)
- [ ] Resource needs identified
- [ ] Named action owners and due dates for all agreed actions

Minutes must be retained as evidence (minimum 3 years).

---

## 6. Review Process Flow

```
6 weeks before review
        │
        ▼
ISMS Owner sends calendar invite to all attendees + prepares input pack
        │
        ▼
2 weeks before review
        │
        ▼
Input pack distributed (review inputs checklist + all reports)
        │
        ▼
Review meeting (60–90 minutes)
        │
        ▼
Draft minutes produced within 5 business days
        │
        ▼
Minutes reviewed and approved by CEO
        │
        ▼
Action items logged in management review action tracker
        │
        ▼
Actions monitored by ISMS Owner (monthly check-in)
        │
        ▼
Status of actions = mandatory input to next review
```

---

## 7. Evidence Retention

| Record | Retention | Location |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Management review minutes | 3 years | ISMS document store |
| Input pack (KPI reports, audit summary, risk register snapshot) | 3 years | ISMS document store |
| Action tracker | Ongoing | ISMS document store |
| Attendance list | 3 years | ISMS document store |

**Auditor note:** Certification body auditors look for: (1) CEO signature or name on attendance list, (2) all 9.3.2 inputs addressed in minutes, (3) named actions with due dates, (4) evidence actions from prior review were completed.
